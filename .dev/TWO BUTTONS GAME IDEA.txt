TWO BUTTONS
[ 1 ] [ 2 ]

-List of 20 inputs
Initially the first game:
    - 10 [1]
    - 10 [2]
-> Odds start at 50/50 for [1] and [2]

LOOP
1. Game displays current odds
2. Player bets money
3. Player chooses to press [1] or [2]
4. Game decides outcome based on current odds
5. Payout/loss -> payout is multiplied by odds
6. Game logs and saves the outcome into list of inputs, replacing one previous input from end of list
7. Game calculates new odds for [1] and [2] based on new list of 20 inputs
8. Repeat
