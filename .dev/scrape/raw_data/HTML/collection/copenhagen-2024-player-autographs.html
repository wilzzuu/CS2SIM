Copenhagen 2024 Player Autographs

Copenhagen 2024 Player Autographs were released for the PGL Copenhagen CS2 Major in March 2024. Apply these stickers to show support for the pros who battled at this landmark early CS2 event.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are Copenhagen 2024 autographs?
Copenhagen 2024 Player Autographs feature signatures from pros at the PGL Copenhagen Major in March 2024, one of the first Majors held under CS2.

Who won the Copenhagen 2024 Major?
NAVI won the PGL Copenhagen Major 2024. Check autograph sticker prices on SteamAnalyst across 30+ marketplaces.

Are Copenhagen 2024 stickers valuable?
As early CS2-era Major stickers, they carry significance for collectors. Track price trends on SteamAnalyst.
