The Anubis Collection

The Anubis Collection was introduced alongside the Anubis map’s addition to CS2’s competitive rotation. Featuring golden hieroglyphics and desert motifs, these skins bring ancient Egyptian grandeur to CS2.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Anubis Collection in CS2?
The Anubis Collection features Egyptian-themed weapon skins inspired by the Anubis map, with golden hieroglyphics and desert motifs.

How do I get Anubis Collection skins?
If Anubis is in the current active map pool, these skins may drop at the end of matches. They can also be purchased from the Steam Market and third-party marketplaces.

Are Anubis Collection skins valuable?
As a collection with unique Egyptian designs, Anubis skins have collector appeal. Check prices on SteamAnalyst.
