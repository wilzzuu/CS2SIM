The Cobblestone Collection

The Cobblestone Collection is themed around the medieval castle-inspired Cobblestone map, which was removed from the competitive map pool. Home to the legendary AWP | Dragon Lore, it is one of the most valuable collections in Counter-Strike history. These skins no longer drop and can only be obtained from the Steam Market or third-party marketplaces.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Cobblestone Collection in CS2?
The Cobblestone Collection features skins themed around the medieval Cobblestone map. It’s home to the AWP Dragon Lore — one of the most valuable skins in Counter-Strike history.

Why is the AWP Dragon Lore so expensive?
The AWP Dragon Lore is extremely rare. It originally only dropped from Cobblestone souvenir packages at Majors, and since the map was removed from the pool, no new souvenir packages are being created. A Factory New Dragon Lore can sell for tens of thousands of dollars.

Can I still get Cobblestone Collection drops?
No. Cobblestone was removed from the map pool and is no longer played at Majors, so no new drops or souvenir packages are being produced. These skins can only be purchased from the Steam Market or third-party marketplaces.
