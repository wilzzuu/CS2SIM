The Kilowatt Collection

The Kilowatt Collection arrived in June 2024 with the Kilowatt Case. It electrifies CS2 with energy-themed skins including the AK-47 Inheritance and AWP Chrome Cannon.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Kilowatt Collection in CS2?
The Kilowatt Collection arrived in June 2024 with the Kilowatt Case. It features energy-themed weapon skins with neon circuits, lightning accents, and high-voltage designs.

What are the most valuable Kilowatt Collection skins?
The AK-47 Inheritance and AWP Chrome Cannon are the most sought-after skins. Check current prices across 30+ marketplaces on SteamAnalyst.

How do I get Kilowatt Collection skins?
Open Kilowatt Cases with a key, or purchase skins directly from the Steam Market or third-party marketplaces.
