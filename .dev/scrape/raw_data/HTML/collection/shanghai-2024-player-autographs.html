Shanghai 2024 Player Autographs

Shanghai 2024 Player Autographs were released for the Perfect World Shanghai CS2 Major in December 2024. These autograph stickers let you showcase your favorite pro players from this historic Asian Major.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are Shanghai 2024 autographs?
Shanghai 2024 Player Autographs feature signatures from pros at the Perfect World Shanghai Major in December 2024, the first CS2 Major held in Asia.

Who won the Shanghai 2024 Major?
Check the tournament results and autograph sticker prices on SteamAnalyst across 30+ marketplaces.

Why is the Shanghai Major significant?
The Shanghai Major was the first CS2 Major held in Asia, marking Counter-Strike’s expansion into the Chinese market through the Perfect World partnership.
