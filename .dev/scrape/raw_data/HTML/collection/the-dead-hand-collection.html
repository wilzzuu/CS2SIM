The Dead Hand Collection

The Dead Hand Collection is CS2’s second Sealed Terminal release, launched March 11–12, 2026. It features 17 community-designed weapon skins (all with StatTrak variants) and 22 brand-new glove finishes — the first new gloves added to Counter-Strike since Operation Broken Fang in December 2020. Flagship skins include the Covert AWP | Queen’s Gambit and Glock-18 | Fully Tuned.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Dead Hand Collection in CS2?
The Dead Hand Collection is CS2’s second Sealed Terminal release, launched March 11–12, 2026. It features 17 community-designed weapon skins with StatTrak variants and 22 brand-new glove finishes — the first new gloves added to Counter-Strike since Operation Broken Fang in December 2020.

How does the Dead Hand Terminal work?
The Dead Hand Terminal is a container that does not require a key. When you unseal it, Valve presents a random Dead Hand item at a set price. You can accept the offer or re-roll up to 5 times. If you decline all 5 offers, the Terminal is destroyed. Accepted items arrive with an Original Owner Certificate.

What are the new gloves in the Dead Hand Collection?
The Dead Hand Collection introduces 22 new glove finishes across three types: 7 Sport Gloves, 7 Specialist Gloves, and 8 Driver Gloves. Popular picks include Driver Gloves Brocade Crane and Seigaiha. Gloves are not available with StatTrak.

What are the most valuable Dead Hand Collection skins?
The two Covert (Red) items are the most valuable: AWP Queen’s Gambit with golden engravings and Glock-18 Fully Tuned with an anime-style design. The Classified AK-47 Crane Flight is also highly sought after. For gloves, Driver Gloves Brocade Crane and Seigaiha command premiums.
