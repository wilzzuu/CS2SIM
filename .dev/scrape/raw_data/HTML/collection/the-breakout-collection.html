The Breakout Collection

The Breakout Collection arrived in July 2014 with Operation Breakout. Featuring instant classics like the M4A1-S Cyrex, it was one of the first community-driven operations. As an older collection, Breakout skins often hold their value well.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Breakout Collection in CS2?
The Breakout Collection arrived in July 2014 with Operation Breakout. It features classic skins like the M4A1-S Cyrex and was one of the first community-driven operations.

Are Breakout Collection skins valuable?
As an older collection with limited supply, many Breakout skins hold their value well. Check current prices on SteamAnalyst.

What is the most popular Breakout skin?
The M4A1-S Cyrex became an instant classic and remains one of the most recognizable skins from this era.
