Missing Link Community Charm Collection

The Missing Link Community Charm Collection features community-designed weapon charms for CS2. These collectible charms dangle from your weapons with creative community-made designs.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are CS2 weapon charms?
Weapon charms are small collectible accessories that dangle from your weapon during gameplay. They add a personal touch without affecting gameplay.

How do I equip a weapon charm?
Go to your inventory, select a charm, and apply it to any weapon. Charms can be moved between weapons.

Are weapon charms tradable?
Yes, CS2 weapon charms are fully tradable on the Steam Market and third-party marketplaces.
