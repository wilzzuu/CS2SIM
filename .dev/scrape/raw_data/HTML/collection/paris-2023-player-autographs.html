Paris 2023 Player Autographs

Paris 2023 Player Autographs were released for the BLAST.tv Paris Major in May 2023 — the last CS:GO Major before the transition to Counter-Strike 2. These autographs hold special historical significance.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are Paris 2023 autographs?
Paris 2023 Player Autographs feature signatures from pros who competed at the BLAST.tv Paris Major in May 2023 — the last CS:GO Major before the transition to Counter-Strike 2.

Why are Paris 2023 stickers historically significant?
The Paris Major was the final Major held under CS:GO before the game transitioned to CS2, making these stickers the last of the CS:GO era.

Who won the Paris 2023 Major?
Vitality won the BLAST.tv Paris Major 2023, with ZywOo leading the team to victory on home soil in France.
