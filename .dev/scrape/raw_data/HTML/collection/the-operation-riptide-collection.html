The Operation Riptide Collection

The Operation Riptide Collection was released alongside Operation Riptide in September 2021. Featuring ocean and wave-themed designs, this collection includes diverse weapon finishes ranging from tropical vibes to deep-sea aesthetics.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Operation Riptide Collection?
The Operation Riptide Collection was released in September 2021 alongside Operation Riptide. It features ocean and wave-themed weapon designs with coastal aesthetics.

Can I still get Operation Riptide skins?
Yes, Operation Riptide skins are tradable and available on the Steam Market and third-party marketplaces, even though the operation itself has ended.

What are the rarest Operation Riptide skins?
