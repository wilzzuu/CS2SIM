Why is the M4A4 | Howl classified as Contraband?

The M4A4 | Howl is the only Contraband-grade item in CS2. Valve removed it from all future case drops, making existing copies permanently limited in supply. This unique history makes it one of the most iconic and valuable skins in CS2.
